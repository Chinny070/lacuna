# Peer community benchmark -- July 2025 to March 2026

Three comparable open-source communities of similar size, same measurement
method, same nine-month period. Used to test whether the subject community's
churn level is explained by market-wide conditions.

| Community | Members | Mean churn Jul-Dec 2025 (bps) | Mean churn Jan-Mar 2026 (bps) |
|-----------|---------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Peer A    | 3900    | 3380                          | 3395                          |
| Peer B    | 4400    | 3450                          | 3440                          |
| Peer C    | 4050    | 3410                          | 3420                          |

Peer mean: 3413 bps for Jul-Dec 2025 and 3418 bps for Jan-Mar 2026. Peer
churn is flat across the whole period, within 40 bps of the subject
community's own flat level.

No market-wide shock, platform policy change, or sector-wide event was
recorded in this period for any of the three peers.
