community-membership products, published as a LACUNA smoke-test fixture.

The panel median tracks a slow decline of roughly 60-70 bps per month
across this window, consistent across all three months with no single-month
discontinuity. No panel member reported a platform-wide shock, market
disruption, or comparable event during this period.

This file is a fixed test fixture published for a LACUNA protocol smoke
test. It is not a real market benchmark and must not be used as evidence in
a real performance agreement.
